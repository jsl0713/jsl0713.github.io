About Me

I am an assistant professor in the Operations Research and Industrial Engineering program at the University of Texas at Austin. Prior to joining UT Austin, I was a postdoctoral researcher in the H. Milton Stewart School of Industrial and Systems Engineering at Georgia Tech. I earned my Ph.D. in Industrial Engineering and Operations Research from the University of California, Berkeley, and hold both a B.S. and M.S. in Industrial Engineering from Seoul National University.

Research Interests

My research goals center on developing strong formulations and efficient solution approaches for mixed-integer optimization problems, particularly by exploiting special problem structures. Key areas of interest include:

  • Convexification of mixed-integer convex optimization
  • Tractable distributed MIP algorithms
  • Applications in statistical learning and control optimization
  • Combined framework of optimization and machine learning
  • Optimization under uncertainty
I am seeking self-motivated graduate students and postdocs with strong mathematical foundations.
